# Setting the Stage

- A standalone product built on top of the Eivo Platform
- User-created complete educational works — interactive and multimedia by design
- Full platform capabilities: gamification, competitive mechanics and social features
- Three components: EiBook Studio (creation), EiBook (the work), EiBook Portal (distribution and execution)

# EiBook Studio

**AI-agentic creation and management environment**

- Creators describe vision through conversational dialogue — AI generates the complete EiBook specification
- Iterative process: generate → review → refine until the EiBook matches intent
- Manages full EiBook lifecycle: accessibility settings, publishing, analytics (reads, completions, results)
- Leverages Eivo Platform capabilities: Editorial Services, practice and evaluation systems

---

<!-- _header: "EIBOOK PORTAL" -->
<!-- _footer: "DISCOVER · ACCESS · EXECUTE" -->

# EiBook Portal

**Public marketplace for discovering and accessing published EiBooks**

- Browsing, search and discovery across topics, subjects and learning objectives
- Access management: public EiBooks freely available, private EiBooks restricted
- Runtime environment: loads the EiBook specification and delivers the full learning experience — content, activities, evaluations and interactive features

---

<!-- _header: "CONTENT MODERATION" -->
<!-- _footer: "REVIEW · VALIDATE · APPROVE" -->

# Content Moderation

**Required step before publication to the Portal**

- Ensures quality, safety and compliance
- Validates content, interactions, gamification rules and social features
- Controls what reaches the public marketplace
- Gate between EiBook creation and public distribution

---

<!-- _header: "EIVO: THE MISSING PIECES" -->
<!-- _footer: "STUDIO · PORTAL · FUTURE" -->

# Eivo: The Missing Pieces

**EiBook Studio and Portal are new product surfaces — not existing Eivo verticals**

**Studio requires:**
- AI-agentic authoring interface
- Full lifecycle management and analytics

**Portal requires:**
- Marketplace infrastructure and access management
- Runtime execution environment for EiBooks

**Future capabilities:**
- Agent-driven content creation
- Adaptive difficulty, multi-modal content (video, audio, simulations)
- Collaborative authoring workflows
